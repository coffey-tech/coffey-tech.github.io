# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

Dingo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- All recipes and meal plans you create are stored locally on your device.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- No account is required, and no data is sent to us or to third parties.

If you delete the app, all of its data is removed from your device.
